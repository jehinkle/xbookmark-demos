A Phase 1 Dose-Escalation Study of XYZ-101 in Adults With Relapsed Solid Tumors

Registry ID: XT-2026-00101 · Sponsor: Example Biotherapeutics · Status: Recruiting

Brief Summary

The purpose of this study is to find the maximum tolerated dose and recommended phase 2 dose of XYZ-101, an investigational oral kinase inhibitor, and to describe its safety, pharmacokinetics and preliminary anti-tumor activity in adults whose solid tumors have relapsed after standard therapy.

Study Design

Study typeInterventional
PhasePhase 1
AllocationNon-randomized
DesignBayesian optimal interval (BOIN) dose escalation, 6 dose levels
Enrollment48 (estimated)

Primary Outcome Measures

  1. Incidence of dose-limiting toxicities (DLTs) during Cycle 1 [Time frame: 28 days]
  2. Incidence of treatment-emergent adverse events, graded by CTCAE v5.0 [Time frame: up to 2 years]

Eligibility Criteria

Inclusion:

  • Age 18 years or older
  • Histologically confirmed relapsed or refractory solid tumor
  • ECOG performance status 0–1

Exclusion:

  • Active brain metastases
  • QTcF > 470 ms
